VOLTRING Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 October 2026. Developer: sarmadcodes. Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL

VOLTRING ("the game") is an offline arcade game for Android. This policy explains what data the game handles.

The game itself collects nothing

VOLTRING has no account, no login and no server. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal information, and the game doesn't send your scores or gameplay anywhere.

Your best score, your top 10 runs and your settings (sound, music, haptics) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or using Settings > Reset records deletes them.

The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which is the only part of the game that uses the internet. Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information to serve, personalise (where you consent) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Your consent choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before showing personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown where applicable). If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data on your phone is removed with Settings > Reset records or by uninstalling the game. Advertising data is held by Google and governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

VOLTRING isn't directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone.
