VOLTRING Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 October 2026. Developer: sarmadcodes. Contact: sarmadsuper3@gmail.com

VOLTRING ("the game") is an offline arcade game for Android with no ads. This policy explains what data the game handles: none leaves your phone.

We collect nothing

VOLTRING has no account, no login and no server. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal information, and the game doesn't send your scores or gameplay anywhere.

Your best score, your top 10 runs and your settings (sound, music, haptics) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or using Settings > Reset records deletes them.

The game contains no ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and doesn't use your advertising ID.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data on your phone is removed with Settings > Reset records or by uninstalling the game.

Children

VOLTRING collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sarmadsuper3@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or an online feature are added), we'll update this page and the effective date above.
